### 🧬 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer** and **Doctoral Researcher** specializing in the convergence of high-performance backend systems, mathematical physics, and deep learning. Currently pursuing my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering at **UNAM**, my academic work focuses on the application of **Neural Networks and Deep Learning** for structural detection in high-dimensional **3D LiDAR point clouds**. 

In the industry, I engineer production-ready distributed systems, scalable data pipelines, and AI-driven parsing architectures.

- 🔭 **Current Focus:** 3D Deep Learning architectures, physical-force data models, and high-dimensional spatial data analysis for archaeological detection (Tzintzuntzan protocol).
- ⚙️ **Production Stack:** Advanced backend architectures utilizing Scala, Java (Spring Boot), TypeScript, and high-performance computation in Fortran 90 and CUDA.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** 3D point cloud classification, numerical methods, vector field simulations, and building highly accurate parsing engines for complex production environments.
